### Changed

- `SUPPORTED_SPEC_VERSIONS` admits runtimes `1014015` through `1014020`, and
  `SUPPORTED_SPEC_VERSION_RANGE` ends at `1014020`. They add the per-miner capacity reservations,
  the model weights lock, the VRF committee seed and the D-0544 unsigned-open tariff. The wire
  bytes the SDK signs are unchanged.

### Fixed

- Faucet POSTs are no longer automatically retried after ambiguous outcomes. Grant
  confirmation no longer equates a sufficient balance with an attributable grant;
  QuickStart stops on an unknown result instead of silently continuing.

- Preserve an unknown cooperative settlement outcome when finalized-event polling
  reaches its deadline, preventing a competing force settlement or timeout refund.

### Added

- `computeChannel.readMinerCapacity` reads a miner's D-0542 capacity reservations at the
  finalized head and returns its live accepted cap, reserved total, the rate the next open would
  reserve, and whether it fits; `undefined` on a runtime without the storage. `minerCapacity`,
  `reservationRateForSessions` and `reservationRateUpdate` are the pure rules behind it.

- `fundFromFaucetDetailed` distinguishes refused from unknown requests. The current
  faucet protocol lacks an authenticated request-to-transaction binding, so even a
  successful response remains unknown. `waitForAvailableBalance` explicitly checks
  balance; the older boolean helpers remain as deprecated compatibility aliases.

- `SubmitResult.blockHash` names the finalized block when the submitter waits for finality;
  `settlementArgs` and `mapChainEvents` are exported for callers that hold a signer lane.
- `verifyMirror` accepts `revision`, pinning a whole-repo Hugging Face check to that
  commit instead of resolving `main`.
