### Added

- First versioned `da-validator` image for `linux/amd64`.
- `submit --inflight <1-4>` (default 2) uploads that many chunks at once and reads and encodes
  the next one meanwhile. Each provider still gets one shard PUT at a time.
- `submit --provider-fail-limit <K>` (default 8) stops sending shards to a provider after K
  refused PUTs in a row for the rest of the run, unless fewer than 3 providers would remain.
  Its shards stay unconfirmed and the custody check after the push still decides.
- Opt-in source pull (#4263). `serve --self-ingest`
  accepts a publisher-signed source hint at `PUT /blob/{c}/source`. It then builds this
  validator's shards of an assigned blob from the hint's mirrors with HTTP Range reads. Each
  chunk is checked against its manifest leaf before its shard is stored, and a refused chunk
  tries the next mirror. Chunks no mirror serves are left to peer re-replication.
  `--self-ingest-hosts` (default `huggingface.co,hf.co`) bounds the hosts it may contact, and
  `--self-ingest-concurrency` (default 2) the chunks fetched at once.
- `submit --source-files <table> --source-mirror <url>` offers that hint first, waits while
  providers self-ingest (`--source-stall-secs`, default 600), then pushes only what they lack.
- A custody worker submits `DaRegistry::confirm_custody` once this validator's assigned index
  of a custody-latched blob is custody-pinned. It is inert while the chain latches no blob.

### Changed

- `submit` logs why a provider refuses a shard (its HTTP status or error) when it starts
  refusing, and bounds each request (connect 10 s, 150 s in all, past the validator's own
  60 s admission plus 60 s body deadline).

- A blob may have up to 65,536 chunks (4 TiB at the unchanged 64 MiB chunk size), up from
  16,384 (1 TiB). The manifest body limit grows to 2 MiB to match. A validator on an older
  image rejects these manifests, so roll every validator before such a blob is registered.

### Fixed

- `retrieve` no longer drops shards whose body takes longer than 30 s to arrive. The client bounds
  connect (10 s) and each read (30 s) instead of the whole request, so `--chunk` spot checks pass
  behind the public DA edge.
